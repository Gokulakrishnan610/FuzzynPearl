Good to know

How long does a bouquet take?

Most are made within 3 to 5 days of confirming your order. Need it sooner? Say so when you message and I'll tell you honestly if it's possible.

Can I choose the colours?

Yes. Every piece is made to order, so colours, ribbon, wrap and the little centre details are yours to pick. You can even ask for a name or message on the ribbon.

Why does handmade cost more than a shop bouquet?

Each flower is twisted and stitched by hand, and the heart bouquet alone has twelve of them. You're paying for hours of work and something that lasts years, not a week.

How do I look after it?

Keep it away from damp and direct sun, dust it gently with a soft brush, and swap the batteries in the light-up ones when the glow fades. Pipe cleaners can flatten if squashed, so stand it upright.

Do you deliver?

Pickup is free. Delivery can be arranged, and the cost depends on where you are. Just ask when you order.

How do I pay?

We confirm the design and price on WhatsApp first. An advance secures your date, and the rest is paid when you collect it.
